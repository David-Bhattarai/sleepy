# MindBridge - AI Mental Health Chatbot
## Machine Learning Powered Therapeutic Companion
### *NCIT Final Year Project - Production Ready System*

**Repository**: MindBridge AI Mental Health Platform (Originally AURA)  
**Creator & Owner**: David Bhattarai (davidbhattarai02@gmail.com)  
**Institution**: Nepal College of Information Technology (NCIT)  
**Project Status**: Complete & Production Ready  
**GitHub**: https://github.com/David-Bhattarai/sleepy

MindBridge is a comprehensive AI-powered mental health platform designed to provide therapeutic support through intelligent conversation and emotion detection. Originally developed as "AURA ML Mental Health Chatbot" by David Bhattarai in January 2026 as a Final Year Project at Nepal College of Information Technology (NCIT), this project was later renamed to "MindBridge" to better reflect its comprehensive mental health ecosystem with advanced machine learning capabilities.

**Project Development History**: Initially created by David Bhattarai as "AURA" - an innovative AI-powered mental health chatbot. After developing the complete system with advanced features, the project was renamed to "MindBridge" to represent the comprehensive platform that bridges the gap between technology and mental health support.

The platform features an AI chatbot trained on therapeutic conversation patterns achieving  in intent recognition, real-time facial emotion detection with , video consultation system, mood tracking, and comprehensive administrative tools.
